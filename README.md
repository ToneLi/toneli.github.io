I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [the University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://www.umass.edu/). My research focuses on large language models, agentic retrieval, deep research, reasoning, and AI for biomedicine.

### General AI: Agents, Retrieval, and Reasoning

I am particularly interested in how LLM-based agents can reason, plan, retrieve and integrate information, use external tools, and adapt their behavior over long-horizon interactions. My research aims to build agentic AI systems that are both effective and efficient, maintaining task effectiveness while reducing unnecessary reasoning, retrieval, and tool-use costs. More broadly, I study how agents can dynamically allocate computation, manage knowledge, and adjust their strategies to balance solution quality with inference and interaction efficiency.

### AI for Biomedicine and Healthcare

My research interests also lie in AI for biomedicine and healthcare, with a focus on large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, clinical prediction, biomedical information extraction, medical reasoning, and knowledge graph learning. My work covers applications including cancer, opioid overdose risk prediction, medical question answering, clinical information extraction, nutrition, and other healthcare applications. More broadly, I am interested in developing reliable and efficient AI systems that can represent, integrate, and reason over complex biomedical and clinical information to support biomedical research, clinical diagnosis and decision-making.
